English

Tap Tap VIBE (“the app”) is developed by Vladyslav Panchenko (“we”). We respect your privacy. The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. We do not have servers for the app at all.

Where your progress is stored

Your game progress, scores, purchased items and Beats balance are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage, run by Apple), so the game shows the same progress on all your devices with the same Apple ID. Settings stay on each device. We never receive this data and have no access to your iCloud. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device.

Motion sensor

The app reads the accelerometer only during gameplay to detect “shake” notes. Motion data is processed instantly on the device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see your Game Center nickname and score. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID or contact details.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information, name or Apple ID.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and off by default. They are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push tokens or data are sent to us.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
